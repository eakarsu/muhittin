# PHASE 1 — CRITICAL FIXES (DO FIRST)

These affect credibility and should be fixed immediately.

---

## 1. Fix Brand Name Consistency

### Issue:

Site shows mixed identity:

•⁠  ⁠Muhittin
•⁠  ⁠Multiverse Consulting Group
•⁠  ⁠Business Growth Platform

This creates confusion and hurts trust.

### Developer Tasks:

Standardize everywhere:

Replace with:

*Multiverse Consulting Group*

Update:

Meta titles
Page titles
Browser tab title
SEO metadata
Article templates
Footer copyright
Schema name

### Required Title Format:

Homepage:


Multiverse Consulting Group | Global Advisory Platform


Article pages:


[Article Title] | Insights | Multiverse Consulting Group


### Acceptance criteria:

No page contains:
Muhittin
Business Growth Platform

Unless intentional.

---

## 2. Fix Main Navigation Structure

### Issue:

Too many top-level options reduces clarity.

### Required Navigation Structure:

*Main menu:*

Partnerships

Max 7 primary menu items.
